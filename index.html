// who are we

gen z,
explained.

born 1997–2012. the first generation to grow up with smartphones as a given. digital natives who speak internet fluently, care deeply, and multitask across seventeen tabs while a cat judges them on Zoom.

📱

digital natives

gen z grew up with web 2.0 and digital technology as an established commodity — online video, gaming, and social media were never novelties, just reality.

🧠

mental health aware

greater awareness and open conversation around mental health distinguishes zoomers. they talk about it. they seek help. they check in on each other (and their cats).

🌍

politically active

gen z has been active in politics around the world — showing up, speaking up, and making noise on the issues that matter most to their future.

🎮

play-first culture

online gaming, streaming, and content creation are native languages. entertainment is participatory, not passive. and sometimes the cat runs across the keyboard mid-stream.

⏳

delayed gratification

research shows zoomers are actually better at delaying gratification than teens from the 1960s — patient, strategic, and focused on long-term goals.

🌀

nostalgia coded

nostalgia is a defining theme of zoomer culture in the 2010s and 2020s. y2k, flip phones, early youtube — it all hits different in retrospect.

// the thesis

why cats
on zoom, tho?

no fr — there's a reason.

  • 01

    anxiety buffer IRL

    gen z experiences higher reported anxiety and mental health challenges. having a cat in frame (or just nearby) during calls is scientifically linked to lower stress and cortisol. that's just facts.

  • 02

    virtual connection, physical warmth

    zoomers grew up digital but crave genuine connection. cats bridge the gap — a living, purring presence that grounds you when the world is 100% screens.

  • 03

    perfect content, naturally

    gen z communicates through memes, clips, and shared cultural moments. a cat walking across a keyboard during a presentation? peak content. zero effort required.

  • 04

    authentic over performative

    zoomers reject the polished persona. a cat appearing uninvited on your call is chaotic, real, and deeply relatable — exactly the vibe the generation values most.

  • 05

    nostalgia + internet culture DNA

    cat content is foundational internet culture — from early youtube to tiktok. for zoomers raised online, cats on screen is both a callback and a constant. it never gets old.
